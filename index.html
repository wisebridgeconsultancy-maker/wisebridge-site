Founder — Angupriya Bharathangam

AB
FOUNDER
The person behind Wisebridge

Angupriya Bharathangam

Started Wisebridge after watching too many students pay agents who pushed whichever university paid the highest commission — often the wrong fit, sometimes the wrong country entirely. Wisebridge exists to be the second opinion nobody had to pay for.

The bigger goal isn't just admissions — it's a community where a student in their first winter in Berlin can help the next one figure out heating, hostels, and homesickness before it becomes a crisis.

What we help with

Come in with one question or all of them. Most students start with one and stay for the rest.

01

Test prep

IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, GRE, SAT — recorded sessions, study material, and full-length mocks so the score isn't the thing holding you back.

02

Country & course match

An honest read on where you actually stand, then a shortlist of countries and courses that fit your goals, budget, and profile — not a sales pitch for one country.

03

Profile & applications

Resume, relevant projects, SOP that sounds like you and not a template, LORs, transcripts, deadlines tracked so nothing slips.

04

Loans & scholarships

Which lenders actually work with your profile, what paperwork they'll ask for, and which scholarships you're realistically eligible for.

05

Visa & pre-departure

Document checklist, mock visa interviews, and the practical stuff after the visa lands — flights, first accommodation, what to actually pack.

06

Once you're there

Internship leads and a growing alumni network for when you need someone who's already sat in that classroom.

You're not figuring this out alone

Every Wisebridge student joins a running community of people who've already been through it — ask about a course, a college, a hostel, or just what to eat when nothing feels like home.
